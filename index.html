AgentHalo

A personal trust field for AI agents — the layer where an agent carries memory, identity, permissions, and consent across every device you own. Not a chatbot. Not a super app.

  • Consent receipts and short-lived delegation grants with purpose, scope, and expiry
  • Append-only audit trail of intents, policy changes, and agent outcomes
  • HaloProof — signed proofs that verify what an agent was allowed to do, and did
  • Nine modules: Vault, ID, Consent, Policy, Delegation, Registry, Resolver, Connect, Audit
