03 / A little context

Two decades of building.
Still asking what’s next.

I’m Mahesh, founder and CEO of Unify Dynamics. For more than twenty years, I’ve worked on AI and CRM platforms for governments and global enterprises, including Microsoft, PIMCO and the NYPD.

Today, my work centres on the trust layer for the internet of AI agents: how they identify themselves, receive permission and remain accountable. I contribute to MIT’s Project NANDA and OWASP AIVSS, and have invested in more than 100 startups across AI, automation and enterprise software.
